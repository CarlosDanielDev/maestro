The owner's goal for this whole effort is "same autonomy, fewer tokens" — and today that claim cannot be measured: the 2026-11-27 benchmark needed hand-parsed result events to learn that a setwave seat costs $0.44 and a maestro seat $0.023 for the same task (18×), and maestro's own numbers were wrong where they existed (PR body $0.00; `total_cost_usd` resetting between runs — patched by {{p3}}). Nobody can attribute cost per seat, per role, or per wave, so "the judge costs too much" or "the richer prompt paid for itself" are unanswerable. This slice makes the token goal a surface instead of a hunch: the Usage views (#1289, shipped in v0.34.0) gain per-seat and per-wave cost breakdowns fed by the lifecycle record ({{e2}}) and by the per-session `cost_usd` the record already carries (`crates/maestro-core/src/session/types.rs:401`).

## ADR stub

Where grouping lives: computed on read from the lifecycle records (recommended — no second store) vs pre-aggregated at write time (rejected: a second source of truth for the same number). One fork.

## Out of scope

- Token-level (not dollar) accounting beyond what providers already report.
