Maestro's loop ends at "gates pass + PR open" (`check_completions`, `crates/maestro-tui/src/tui/app/completion_pipeline.rs:13`) — after that, the human is the only reviewer. Grep across the engine finds no judge/verdict concept at all, while the setwave engine's judge is merge-blocking and measured busy: 1,414 judge invocations in 2 months on this machine, catching blocking defects nearly every wave (recorded examples: a retry that wiped worktrees, GitHub writes firing unarmed, keys acting on hidden sessions). The council's condition for building it natively: LAST, opt-in, and only after telemetry ({{e8}}) can price it — setwave's seats cost 18× maestro's ($0.44 vs $0.023 per issue, measured), and the judge is where that money goes; copying it blind would betray the token goal.

## ADR stub

Judge as maestro session vs subprocess of the completion pipeline. Recommendation: a session (reuses provider plumbing, cost accounting, and the lifecycle record; the pipeline stays deterministic). One fork.
